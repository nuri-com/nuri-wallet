# Nuri 0.96.1: your total first

**Web release candidate. OTA and store publication are not included.**

Nuri opens on your total balance, with Bitcoin, Cash, and Card & Banking underneath. Your starting point stays put when balances refresh. Choose a group to open its accounts, or swipe through the carousel yourself.

## What changes

- **Start with the whole picture.** Total Balance is the initial and reset view, instead of jumping to the account with the highest balance.
- **Find accounts in familiar pairs.** Bitcoin and Lightning, Euro and Dollar, VISA and IBAN have a fixed order. Card and banking funds count once in the overview.
- **Identify your app in Support.** The loaded release appears next to Logout. Share Info remains an explicit action, and Logout keeps its confirmation.

No payment routes, signing rules or swap backends are changed by this release. The fresh-Arkade Bitcoin-send fallback and BTC-to-Euro recovery work are not included. This release does not add a red Logout button.
